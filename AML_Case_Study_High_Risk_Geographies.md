# AML Case Study: High-Risk Geographies

## Case Study Data

### Geography: North Africa Region
- **Risk Level:** High
- **Indicators:** Weak regulatory framework, political instability
- **Suspected Activities:** Cross-border money laundering, terrorism financing
- **Transaction Volume:** $2.5B annually
- **Key Findings:** Multiple shell companies, suspicious wire transfers

### Geography: Eastern European Countries
- **Risk Level:** High
- **Indicators:** Inadequate AML controls, high corruption levels
- **Suspected Activities:** Organized crime proceeds, sanctions evasion
- **Transaction Volume:** $4.2B annually
- **Key Findings:** Fake trade invoicing, cryptocurrency conversions

### Geography: Southeast Asian Nations
- **Risk Level:** Critical
- **Indicators:** Limited law enforcement, offshore banking centers
- **Suspected Activities:** Drug trafficking proceeds, human trafficking
- **Transaction Volume:** $6.8B annually
- **Key Findings:** Bulk cash smuggling, hawala networks, trade-based laundering

### Geography: Middle East Jurisdictions
- **Risk Level:** High
- **Indicators:** Complex ownership structures, limited transparency
- **Suspected Activities:** Terrorism financing, sanctions violations
- **Transaction Volume:** $3.1B annually
- **Key Findings:** Informal value transfer systems, dual-use goods smuggling
